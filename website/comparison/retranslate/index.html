YT Localizer vs ReTranslate.ai

A feature-by-feature comparison with a hosted YouTube metadata localization service ReTranslate.ai.

YT Localizer by Vagabondity is a great free alternative to any hosted YouTube solution with a closed code like ReTranslate.ai, based on a comparison below, made in September of 2026.

Comparison between YT Localizer and ReTranslate.ai
Topic YT Localizer ReTranslate.ai
Source code and logic Source-available under the free-to-use license: public and open for inspection and free to run, but redistribution, modified releases, resale, and hosted access are prohibited. Not publicly available - it's impossible to verify what app does, what data it collects and which API calls it makes.
Where it runs Locally on your computer. Hosted third-party service.
Author identity Dmitrii Polovinkin — GitHub ↗, an experienced developer and digital nomad. Unknown.
YouTube channel safety The source contains no calls to YouTube's video-deletion API or anything unrelated to localization. You create the authorization token for your own Google Cloud app, and access keys stay on your device. Details When you log in, ReTranslate Google consent screen explicitly requests permission to see, edit, and permanently delete YouTube videos, ratings, comments, and captions. Its implementation cannot be inspected.
Application subscription There is no subscription, as well as no fees to use the app. You can only donate to support the project if you want to. You have to pay subscription to use the app - Pro at $20/month or Max at $50/month. A free plan exists, but its limits are not enough to localize a single video even partially.
Translation limits No app-imposed limits. Usage is limited only by the translation provider and YouTube's daily API quota. Provider free quotas are usually enough for hundreds of translations. 10 translations to a chosen language per day. Pro lists a “higher daily usage capacity” without specifics; Max is listed as "5x Pro".
Bulk translation and multiple-video selection Automatically loads the list of all your videos. Can translate an entire channel into as many as 50 languages in a single run. No bulk translation on the free plan. A single imported video is localized at a time by manually copying and pasting its URL.
Translation speed Localizing one video into 20 languages and uploading the results to YouTube in one run takes around 25 seconds. One translation for one video even without uploading it to YouTube takes at least five seconds on average, so 20 languages will take at least 100 seconds.
Translation providers You use your own accounts of DeepL (the most powerful translation provider in the world) or Google Cloud Translation (most popular translation tool worldwide powered by Gemini). Mostly only weak LLM models such as GPT-4o mini (used by default, last one is 5.6) or Gemini 2.5 Flash (last one is 3.8 Flash) are available. A model must be selected manually for each language before translation.
Localization monitoring Video-and-language-level progress with individual results and explanations for failed or skipped localizations. Percentage-based progress without explanations when a translation fails or is not uploaded. A run may be marked partially successful while every attempted localization is listed as failed.
Subtitle translation Not yet. Yes.
Channel metadata translation Not yet. Yes.
Playlist metadata translation Not yet. Yes, currently marked Beta.

YT Localizer vs ReTranslate.ai

A feature-by-feature comparison with a hosted YouTube metadata localization service.

Comparison between YT Localizer and ReTranslate.ai
Topic YT Localizer ReTranslate.ai
Source code and logic Source-available under the free-to-use license: public for inspection and free to run, but redistribution, modified releases, resale, and hosted access are prohibited. Not publicly available.
Where it runs Locally on your computer. Hosted third-party service.
Author identity Dmitrii Polovinkin — GitHub ↗ Unknown.
YouTube channel safety The source contains no calls to YouTube's video-deletion API or anything unrelated to localization. You create the authorization token for your own Google Cloud app, and access keys stay on your device. Details The ReTranslate Google consent screen explicitly requests permission to see, edit, and permanently delete YouTube videos, ratings, comments, and captions. Its implementation cannot be inspected.
Application subscription None. Pro at $20/month or Max at $50/month. A free plan exists, but its limits are not enough to partially localize a single video.
Translation limits No app-imposed limits. Usage is limited only by the translation provider and YouTube's daily API quota. Provider free quotas are usually enough for hundreds of translations. 10 translations to one chosen language per day. Pro lists a “higher daily usage capacity” without specifics; Max is listed as five times Pro.
Bulk translation and multiple-video selection Can translate an entire channel into as many as 50 languages in one run. No bulk translation on the free plan. A single imported video is localized at a time by manually pasting its URL.
Translation speed Localizing one video into 20 languages and uploading the results to YouTube takes around 25 seconds in one run. One translation for one video takes at least five seconds on average, without uploading it to YouTube.
Translation providers Your own full DeepL or Google Cloud Translation account. Primarily smaller LLM models such as GPT-4o mini by default or Gemini 2.5 Flash. A model must be selected manually for each language.
Localization monitoring Video-and-language-level progress with individual results and explanations for failed or skipped localizations. Percentage-based progress without explanations when a translation fails or is not uploaded. A run may be marked partially successful while every attempted localization is listed as failed.
Subtitle translation No. Yes.
Channel metadata translation No. Yes.
Playlist metadata translation No. Yes, currently marked Beta.

Local-first YouTube localization

YT Localizer is a free-to-use, source-available tool for translating YouTube video titles and descriptions on your own computer.

It's fully functional already, use it to make hundreds of localizations - you don't need to register or log in to any third-party site (apart from your translation provider) or pay for another subscription. Follow the setup instructions on GitHub to set the app up. Feel free to leave feedback!
